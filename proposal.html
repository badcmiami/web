Home/Design proposal
Website proposal · 2026

A digital presence that matches the center you already run.

Everything you are browsing is the actual prototype: seven working, bilingual, responsive pages built on the new brand system. This page explains the decisions behind it.

Objectives

What the site has to accomplish.

A diagnostic center does not sell a product: it sells trust and speed. The site has to demonstrate both within the first five seconds.

01

Drive calls

The phone is the real conversion channel. It sits in the top bar, the header, every section and pinned on mobile.

02

Remove friction

Preparation, insurance, documents and hours answered before the patient has to ask.

03

Speak two languages

In Hialeah, 95% of residents speak Spanish at home. The site switches language in one click, with no duplicate URLs.

04

Win the referrer

A dedicated section for practices: turnaround times, how to refer and access to the radiologist.

Design direction

Corporate, clean, minimal — with a spine.

We took the European industrial reference you shared as the starting language: generous air, large tight typography, sections that alternate light and dark, and restrained motion that appears on scroll. On that base we applied the new identity.

Grid
1280 px container, fluid gutters, 50/50 and 60/40 splits for narrative blocks.
Rhythm
72–144 px vertical sections on a fluid scale; never two light sections in a row without contrast.
Hierarchy
Eyebrow + headline + lede + action. Three levels, always in the same order.
Photography
Navy/green duotone over real photography of the center; the ones in this demo are temporary vector graphics.
Brand system

Two colors do all the work.

Infinity Navy carries the medical authority; Tahoe Green brings the pulse, the energy and the signal to act. The neutrals are calibrated so both can breathe.

Logo mark

The mark proposal uses the infinity symbol — continuity of care and of the two names — inside a rounded square that works equally at 16 px as a favicon and on a building façade.

Delivered in SVG, PNG and a monochrome version. If a final logo already exists, the system adopts it with no structural changes.

Architecture

Seven pages, each with one clear job.

No page exists “just because”. Each one answers a specific search intent and ends in an action.

  • HomeTrust in 5 seconds + access to everything
  • ServicesSeven studies, use, duration and prep
  • LocationsAddresses, hours, access and directions
  • PatientsPreparation, insurance, forms and FAQ
  • ProvidersHow to refer, turnaround and form
  • AboutStory, values, team and quality
  • ContactAppointment form and direct details

Bilingual strategy

Every string on the site carries its Spanish version alongside the original. The EN/ES button swaps the content instantly, remembers the visitor's preference and updates the language attribute for screen readers.

  • No duplicate pages or /es/ tree to maintain
  • Preference stored between visits
  • For maximum SEO it can migrate to /es/ with hreflang

Try it: the EN/ES button is in the top bar of this very page.

System

Components and motion.

The whole site is assembled from these pieces. Adding a new page needs no new design — you combine existing blocks.

Buttons

Primary navy, green, light and ghost. All with a fill that rises on hover and an arrow that shifts.

Primary AccentGhost

Cards

Hover elevation, a top gradient line and an icon that rotates. Light, dark and flat variants.

Index rows

Editorial list with numbering, an appearing background and a circle that rotates 45°. Ideal for service catalogs.

Accordions & tabs

Animated height, single open panel, keyboard accessible. Used for FAQ and preparation.

Smart header

Transparent over the hero, solid on scroll, with a services mega-menu and a full-screen mobile menu.

Forms

Live validation, error states, visual confirmation and a notice not to send medical information.

A

Scroll reveal

Translate and opacity with staggered delay per group.

B

Counters

Figures that count up on entry, with an ease-out curve.

C

Marquee

Infinite trust strip that pauses on hover.

D

Ken Burns + progress

Slow hero zoom and a reading-progress bar at the top edge.

All motion switches off automatically if the visitor has enabled “reduce motion” in their operating system.

Technical standards

Fast, findable and accessible by design.

Performance

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no external libraries. The entire site weighs less than one unoptimized photo.

SEO

Per-page titles and descriptions, MedicalBusiness structured data with both locations, hours and phone numbers for Google.

Accessibility

AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, ARIA labelling and respect for reduced motion.

Privacy

Forms collect contact details, never clinical information, with an explicit notice and a secure path for real submissions.

Delivery

Four phases from this demo to launch.
