Our story

Twenty-five years caring for the same families.

We began with one machine, two technologists and a simple idea: that no one in Hialeah should have to leave their neighborhood — or their language — to get a reliable diagnosis.

The center

Close to home, held to a large-hospital standard.

Best American Diagnostic Center started as a local answer to a real problem: waiting. A mother with persistent pain should not wait three weeks for an MRI, nor drive forty minutes to get one.

Today we run two Hialeah locations with six diagnostic modalities, a fully bilingual team and board-certified radiologists reading every study. We remain independent — and that independence is exactly what lets us spend twenty minutes with a patient who walked in afraid.

What we stand on

Four commitments we do not negotiate.

These are not slogans. They are internal rules that shape how we hire, how we schedule and how we answer the phone.

Your language

Every conversation, form and instruction available in Spanish and English, without exception.

No long waits

Appointments within the same week and slots held for same-day urgent orders.

Safety first

Lowest necessary dose, implant screening and protocols reviewed by the radiologist.

Family treatment

We explain every step before it happens. No one enters the scanner without knowing what to expect.
