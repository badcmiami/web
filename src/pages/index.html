Why patients choose us

The machine is the easy part. Everything around it is the work.

Authorizations, transportation, fear, language, results that take too long. We designed the center to take each of those frictions off your plate.

We chase the authorization, not you

Our team calls your insurer and your physician's office, confirms coverage and tells you exactly what you will owe before you arrive.

Your language, from the first phone call

Every member of staff — front desk, technologists and coordinators — works in Spanish and English. No one has to bring a relative to translate.

Appointments that respect your day

Real time slots, not a waiting list. And when your physician needs an urgent study, we open same-day capacity.

Results that arrive in time to matter

Reports signed by board-certified radiologists, delivered digitally to your physician — with critical findings called through immediately.

How it works

Three steps between your doctor's order and your answer.

Book

Arrive 15 minutes early with ID, insurance card and your physician's order. Most studies take between 15 and 45 minutes.
